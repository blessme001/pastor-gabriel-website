Ministry Profile

A Life Devoted
to Divine Purpose

Pastor Gabriel Abimbola Adegboye is the Presiding Head Pastor of Worksword of God Church and a passionate servant of God whose ministry has spanned decades of church planting, peace advocacy, and national assignment. His journey began in Warri, Delta State, where he was rooted in faith and first answered the call to plant a church — bringing the gospel to the heart of the Niger Delta.

During the turbulent years of the Niger Delta crisis, Pastor Gabriel stood as a voice for peace, actively advocating for reconciliation among communities torn by conflict. He later received a divine leading to move to Ibadan, where he planted Worksword of God Church — a ministry built on the uncompromising teaching of God's Word. He has now received a fresh assignment to Lagos, where he continues his national calling.

He is the Convener of the Save Nigeria Network, a platform dedicated to national peace, security, and reconciliation among all Nigerians. Through this vision, he has engaged leaders, communities, and believers across the nation, believing that spiritual solutions are the foundation of lasting peace.

A thorough student of the Word, Pastor Gabriel has attended several Bible schools including WOFBI, Gethsemane Prayer School, International School of Ministry, and ECWA Theological Seminary. He is married to Pastor Rosemary Adegboye, and together they are blessed with children.
